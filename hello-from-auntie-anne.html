Ethan, I want to teach you a new code so you have a new secret message spy tool. It's called the Caesar cipher, and it is more than 2,000 years old!

A long time ago, the Roman general Julius Caesar really used this code to send secret battle orders to his army. If an enemy stole the message, they just saw a jumble of letters and couldn't read his military secrets. It works by sliding every letter down the alphabet by the same number of steps.
