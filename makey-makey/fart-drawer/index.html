🔌 How to build it

You need: a Makey Makey, the USB-C adapter, 2 alligator clips, aluminum foil, tape, and a drawer.

Drawer OPEN = quiet 🤫

The two pieces of foil don't touch, so the circuit is open. No sound.

Drawer CLOSED = 💨

The foil on the drawer presses on the foil on the cabinet. The circuit is closed. FART!

  1. Plug the Makey Makey USB cable into the USB-C adapter, then plug the adapter into the iPad. A little light on the Makey Makey turns on.
  2. Fold a piece of foil a few times to make a thick strip, about as big as your finger. Make two of these.
  3. Foil #1 goes on the drawer. Tape it to the back side of the drawer front, the part that bumps into the cabinet when the drawer closes.
  4. Foil #2 goes on the cabinet. Tape it on the edge of the cabinet exactly where foil #1 bumps into it. Close the drawer and check that the two foils squish together.
  5. Clip SPACE (the round spot on the front of the Makey Makey) to foil #1 (the drawer). Leave the wire a little loose so the drawer can still open.
  6. Clip EARTH (the strip along the bottom) to foil #2 (the cabinet).
  7. Tap this page once so the iPad is listening. Then open the drawer and close it. 💨

Not farting? The clips have to bite the foil, not the tape. Tape over the edges of the foil so the shiny middle is still showing. A little crumpled foil ball makes a bump that touches better.
Farting when it's open? The two clips or foils are touching somewhere else. Keep them apart.
Close the drawer gently and keep fingers out of the way! 🖐️

🔧 Sound not working?

Tap each test button. Did you hear it?

    Heard 🎵 but not 💨? The iPad is in Silent mode (see #1 below). Videos and Spotify still play in Silent mode, but game sounds don't.

    Didn't hear either one? Check the volume and Bluetooth (#2 and #3).

    Check these on the iPad

    1. Silent mode (the usual problem!). Swipe down from the top-right corner of the screen. If the 🔔 bell is crossed out, tap it to turn sound on. (Older iPads with a little switch on the side: flip it so no orange shows.)
    2. Volume. Press the volume-up button while you're on this page.
    3. Bluetooth. Make sure the sound isn't going to headphones or a speaker in another room. In Control Center, tap the AirPlay icon on the music box and pick iPad.
    4. Microphone (for recording). In Safari, tap aA or the page menu next to the web address → Website Settings → Microphone → Allow. If the iPad uses Screen Time: Settings → Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions → Microphone → Allow Changes.
    5. Headphones too quiet? Settings → Sounds → Headphone Safety → Reduce Loud Audio (Screen Time may lock this one).
    6. Still stuck? Reload this page, tap the screen once, and try the tests again.
